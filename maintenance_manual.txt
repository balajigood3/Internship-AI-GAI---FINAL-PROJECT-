Industrial Maintenance Manual — Revision 1

Centrifugal Pump P-101
When unusual vibration or abnormal noise is observed, inspect the pump and record vibration readings.
Record operating pressure, flow rate, bearing temperature, and operating conditions.
Do not declare a root cause until an inspection has been completed.

Compressor C-202
The sample maintenance record reports a compressor temperature of 110 degrees Celsius.
This manual does not specify the normal operating temperature or the maximum permitted temperature.
Consult the approved equipment datasheet and site procedure before deciding whether the measured temperature is acceptable.

General maintenance safety
Follow the approved site isolation and lockout/tagout procedure before maintenance work.
Never bypass protective interlocks, guards, alarms, or emergency stop systems.
Only authorized and trained personnel may perform maintenance tasks.

Maintenance reports
A complete report should identify the equipment, observation date, symptoms, operating conditions,
inspection findings, actions taken, and the person or team responsible.
